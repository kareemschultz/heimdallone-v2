The Operations area covers cross-cutting work management and insight.

## Modules

<Cards>
  <Card title="Notifications" href="/docs/operations" description="Per-user inbox, read/unread, soft entity links." />
  <Card title="Helpdesk" href="/docs/operations" description="Requests/tickets that link to other modules, never duplicate them." />
  <Card title="Projects" href="/docs/operations" description="Projects, tasks, milestones, time entries (coordination layer)." />
  <Card title="CRM" href="/docs/operations" description="Leads, customers, deals, project handoff." />
  <Card title="Analytics" href="/docs/operations" description="Executive dashboards (read-only aggregation, role-scoped)." />
  <Card title="Inventory" href="/docs/operations/inventory" description="Ledger-backed stock: catalogue, locations, movements with approval, balances." />
  <Card title="Surveys" href="/docs/operations/surveys" description="Anonymous or identified questionnaires with audience targeting and aggregate results." />
</Cards>

<Callout title="Coordination layers">
  Helpdesk, Projects, and CRM link to other modules through clear seams and never
  mutate them. Analytics is pure read-only aggregation and owns no data.
</Callout>

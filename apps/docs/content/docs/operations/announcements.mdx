Announcements are company-wide notices. Everyone sees the **feed**; owners,
administrators and HR admins can **post and manage** them.

## For everyone — the feed

Open **App → Announcements** (in the **Operate** section of the sidebar). The feed
shows published notices, **pinned items first**, with unread notices highlighted.
Opening a notice marks it read.

## For admins — posting

<Callout type="info" title="Who can post">
  Creating, editing, publishing and archiving announcements is limited to
  **Tenant Owner**, **Tenant Admin** and **HR Admin**. Everyone else has a
  read-only feed. The server enforces this regardless of the UI.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>Click **New announcement**, enter a title and message.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **audience**: everyone, a single department, or a single access role.</Step>
<Step>Optionally **pin** it (keeps it at the top) and choose **Publish now** or save as a draft.</Step>
<Step>Manage existing notices from the **Manage** tab — publish/unpublish, archive.</Step>
</Steps>

## Audience targeting

| Audience | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone | All members of the organization. |
| Department | Members whose employee record is in that department. |
| Role | Members holding that access role (e.g. Manager, Employee). |

Audience matching is resolved on the server from the viewer's own role and
department — it is never trusted from the browser.

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Saved but not visible in anyone's feed. |
| Published | Live in the feed for the matching audience. |
| Archived | Removed from the feed; kept for the record. |

## Notes

- An optional **expiry** date hides an announcement from the feed after it passes.
- Read state is per-person, so unread counts are accurate for each member.
- Surveys (the other half of v1 Communications) are a planned follow-on.

If your account was moved from the previous (v1) system, the **first time you sign
in to Heimdallone v2** you'll see a short welcome notice. This page explains what
it asks and how to act on it.

## The welcome notice

The notice confirms that your account and profile were migrated and asks you to
review your details. **It must be acknowledged before you continue** — it will not
close on its own or by clicking outside it.

You have two choices:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Review my profile">
    Marks the notice as acknowledged and takes you straight to your own profile so
    you can check and update your details.
  </Card>
  <Card title="I understand — continue">
    Marks the notice as acknowledged and closes it. You can review your profile
    later from your profile page.
  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout title="You'll only see it once">
  Once acknowledged, the notice does not appear again.
</Callout>

## What to review

<Steps>
<Step>**Contact details.** Confirm your email, phone, address, and emergency contact are correct.</Step>
<Step>**Tax & statutory details.** Where you're allowed to edit them, check your TIN, NIS, and number of dependent children — these affect your pay.</Step>
<Step>**New features.** Take a look at the modules now available to you (leave, payslips, roster, helpdesk, and more).</Step>
<Step>**Report anything wrong.** If any payroll, leave, or attendance data looks incorrect, contact HR — historical records are preserved exactly, and corrections go through a proper, audited process.</Step>
</Steps>

## Passwords & sign-in

How you sign in depends on how your account was migrated:

- If you used an **email and password** before, the same password continues to
  work — it is never reset or weakened by the migration.
- If your sign-in could not be carried over safely, you'll be guided through a
  **secure password reset / invitation** instead. Your password is never
  fabricated or stored in plain text.
- **Google sign-in** is available only where it has been enabled for your account.

<Callout type="warn" title="No fake accounts">
  The migration never creates placeholder email addresses. If you don't have a
  login yet but should, ask HR — see the
  [admin login checklist](/docs/administration/migration-logins).
</Callout>

This page is the admin overview for getting a biometric / time-attendance terminal
working with Heimdallone. The full how-to lives on
[Biometric devices](/docs/time/biometric-devices).

## The two parts

<Steps>
<Step>**Register the device in Heimdallone.** Go to **App → Attendance → Biometrics & Time Clocks → Devices → Register device**. Copy the **device ID** and the **one-time ingest key**.</Step>
<Step>**Point the on-site agent at Heimdallone.** A small agent on your office network reads the terminal and sends punches in. It is configured with the device ID, the ingest key, and your Heimdallone address.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Secrets stay on the on-site agent">
  The ingest key and any device password live in the on-site agent's local
  configuration only — never in shared documents, tickets, or the app. If a key is
  lost, rotate it on the device screen and update the agent.
</Callout>

## After it's connected

- Confirm a recent successful **last sync** on the device.
- Map any **unmatched punches** to employees so their hours are attributed
  correctly.
- Punches flow into [Attendance](/docs/time/attendance), are validated, and (once
  approved) feed payroll.

## What to check

- The device is **active** and shows a recent sync.
- Every clocking employee has a **device-user mapping**.
- The **unmatched punches** queue is clear.

<Callout title="Where this fits at go-live">
  The device is usually registered during the go-live window — see

</Callout>

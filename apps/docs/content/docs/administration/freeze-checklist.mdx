This page explains, in plain terms, what "going live" means for an organization
moving onto Heimdallone — what to expect, what to confirm inside the app, and who
does what. It is **not** a technical runbook.

<Callout type="info" title="Your administrator runs the final steps">
  The final data load and the switch to the live system are performed by your
  administrator during a planned maintenance window. You don't run any commands —
  this page is so you know what's happening and what to check afterwards.
</Callout>

## Before go-live

A few decisions are confirmed with the owner and HR/payroll leads first:

- **Who has a login**, and whether anyone still needs a real email before go-live
  (no placeholder emails are ever created).
- **Tax/statutory details** (TIN, NIS, dependents) for people whose pay depends on them.
- **Pay rules and shifts** are set up and correct.
- **The biometric device** will be registered during the window (see
  [Biometric devices](/docs/time/biometric-devices)).

## What happens during the window

<Steps>
<Step>The old system is briefly set to read-only so nothing changes mid-move.</Step>
<Step>Your administrator loads the organization's data into Heimdallone and verifies it.</Step>
<Step>Payroll figures are checked against the official rules and the historical record.</Step>
<Step>Sign-in, payroll, and attendance are smoke-tested before anyone is let in.</Step>
<Step>Once everything checks out, the organization is switched over to Heimdallone.</Step>
</Steps>

## What to confirm in the app after go-live

<Steps>
<Step>**Sign in** works for you, and (where applicable) Google sign-in works. Migrated users see a one-time [welcome notice](/docs/administration/first-login).</Step>
<Step>**Employees** are all present, in the right departments, with the right managers — see [Employees](/docs/hr/employees).</Step>
<Step>**Payslips** — historical payslips are present and unchanged; a current draft run matches expectations — see [Payroll runs](/docs/payroll/payroll-runs) and [Payslips](/docs/payroll/payslips).</Step>
<Step>**Attendance** records look right and the **biometric device** shows a recent successful sync with no stuck [unmatched punches](/docs/time/biometric-devices).</Step>
<Step>**Finance** figures (cost reports / trial balance) look sensible — see [Finance](/docs/finance).</Step>
</Steps>

## If something looks wrong

- Don't edit historical records directly. Historical payslips are immutable;
  changes go through a proper, audited [correction](/docs/payroll/corrections).
- Tell your administrator — go-live can be paused or rolled back to the old system
  if a check doesn't pass.
- For migrated logins and access questions, see the
  [migrated logins checklist](/docs/administration/migration-logins).

<Callout title="What gets migrated">
  For the full picture of what data comes across and the safety guarantees, see

</Callout>

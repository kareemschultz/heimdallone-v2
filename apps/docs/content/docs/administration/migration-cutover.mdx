If your organization is moving from a previous system, its data is brought into
Heimdallone before you go live. This page explains **what comes across**, **what to
expect**, and **how to check it in the app**.

<Callout title="Migration principle">
  Legacy data is a **source** and an **intent** — not automatic truth. Historical
  records are preserved exactly; known legacy mistakes are corrected against the
  official rules (for Guyana payroll, the GRA is the source of truth); nothing is
  copied blindly.
</Callout>

## What gets migrated

| Area | What comes across | Where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People & logins | Employees, their roles, and their sign-in accounts (passwords are never reset or weakened; Google sign-in carries over where used) | [Employees](/docs/hr/employees), [Migrated logins](/docs/administration/migration-logins) |
| Pay & history | Historical payslips (kept **immutable**) and the pay setup behind them | [Payslips](/docs/payroll/payslips) |
| Time | Attendance history, shifts, and rosters | [Attendance](/docs/time/attendance) |
| Finance | Chart of accounts, journals, and opening balances | [General ledger](/docs/finance/general-ledger) |
| Devices | The biometric terminal and its punch history | [Biometric devices](/docs/time/biometric-devices) |

## What users should expect

- Migrated employees see a one-time **welcome notice** at first sign-in asking them
  to review their details — see [Your first sign-in](/docs/administration/first-login).
- The **same password** continues to work where sign-in could be carried over
  safely; otherwise the user is guided through a secure reset/invite. **No
  placeholder emails are ever created** — someone with no email simply has no login
  until HR adds one.
- **Historical payslips look exactly as they did** and cannot be edited.

## What admins should verify in the app

<Steps>
<Step>**Employees** — everyone is present, in the right department, with the right manager, and statutory details (TIN/NIS/dependents) where needed. Open **App → Employees**.</Step>
<Step>**Payslips** — historical payslips are present and unchanged. Open **App → Payroll → Payslips**.</Step>
<Step>**Attendance** — recent attendance looks right and the biometric device shows a recent successful sync. Open **App → Attendance**.</Step>
<Step>**Finance** — opening balances and the trial balance look sensible. See [General ledger](/docs/finance/general-ledger).</Step>
<Step>**Logins** — check who has access and who is intentionally no-login. Open **App → Migration status** (HR/admin) — see [Migrated logins](/docs/administration/migration-logins).</Step>
</Steps>

## If something looks wrong

- **Don't edit history directly.** Historical payslips are immutable; fixes go
  through an audited [correction](/docs/payroll/corrections) that records the
  reason and the exact differences.
- **No-login employee who should have access?** Add a real email and invite them —
  never invent a placeholder address.
- **Finance opening balance looks off?** Some legacy entries that didn't balance
  are intentionally left out; your accountant enters a corrected opening entry
  rather than Heimdallone fabricating one.
- For anything that doesn't reconcile, raise it with your administrator before the
  organization is finalized.

An **organization** (workspace) is one company in Heimdallone. This page is for
owners and admins setting one up.

## Workspace switcher & settings

The **workspace switcher** in the sidebar shows your current organization and your
role in it. From there you can open **Workspace settings** and, where you belong to
more than one organization, switch between them.

<Callout type="info" title="Switching organizations">
  Most people belong to a single organization. A cross-organization
  [platform owner](/docs/administration/migration-logins) can switch between several
  from the same switcher.
</Callout>

## The Settings module

Go to **App → Settings** to configure the building blocks the rest of the app uses:

| Tab | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| Departments | Your org structure; employees and managers are assigned to departments |
| Positions | Job positions employees hold |
| Roles | Job roles / titles (this is **not** the same as sign-in permissions — see [Users & roles](/docs/administration/users-and-roles)) |
| Work types | How work is classified |
| Employment types | Full-time, part-time, contractor, etc. |
| Shifts | Working patterns (start/end, minimum hours) used by attendance and roster |
| Holidays | Public holidays, which affect attendance day-types and pay |

## What to check

- Departments and positions reflect your real structure before you add employees.
- Shifts and holidays are set up before attendance and payroll run.
- Currency and pay settings are correct (see [Payroll](/docs/payroll)).

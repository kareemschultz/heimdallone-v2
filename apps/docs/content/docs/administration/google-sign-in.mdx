Heimdallone supports signing in with **Google Workspace** alongside email/password
and passkeys.

## For users

On the sign-in screen, choose **Google Workspace** and continue with your Google
account. If you used Google to sign in to the previous system, the same Google
sign-in continues to work after migration.

## For admins

- Google sign-in is enabled by an administrator at the platform level. The Google
  connection's credentials live in **secure configuration only** — never in the app
  and never in documentation.
- Google sign-in is available like before — it isn't restricted to owners.
- Each person still maps to one member with one access role; signing in with Google
  doesn't change what they can do.

<Callout type="warn" title="Secrets stay out of the app">
  Google client credentials are never shown in the app, stored with employee data,
  or written into help content. They are managed in protected configuration by the
  administrator.
</Callout>

## What to check

- Users who relied on Google before can still sign in with it after go-live.
- The expected redirect for Heimdallone is configured on the Google side (your
  administrator handles this during setup).

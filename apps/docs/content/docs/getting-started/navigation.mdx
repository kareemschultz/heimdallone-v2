The Heimdallone app groups modules into areas in the left sidebar. **What you see
depends on your role** — modules you can't access are hidden, not just disabled.

<Callout title="Role-aware navigation">
  Employees see self-service entries (their payslips, leave, requests). Managers
  additionally see team views. Admins see configuration and org-wide modules.
</Callout>

## Areas

- **People & HR** — employees, contracts, onboarding/offboarding, performance, assets.
- **Time** — attendance, rosters, leave, holidays, biometric devices.
- **Payroll** — runs, payslips, country rules, reconciliation.
- **Finance** — costing, budgets, general ledger.
- **Operate** — helpdesk, projects, notifications.
- **Insights** — analytics dashboards (scoped by role).
- **Administration** — tenant settings, RBAC, audit logs, migration.

<Callout type="info">
  Modules still in design review are labelled <Tag>Preview</Tag> in the sidebar and
  in these docs. Preview data is sample/demo, never presented as live.
</Callout>

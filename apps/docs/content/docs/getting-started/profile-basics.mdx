Your profile holds your personal details, work information, and statutory/banking
data. Some fields you can update yourself; others are managed by HR or Payroll.

## Finding your profile

Go to **App → Employees**, then open your own record. The URL looks like
`/app/employees/$id` where `$id` is your employee record number.

Alternatively, if you were just prompted to review your profile after migrating,
the first-login notice takes you there directly.

## What you can edit yourself

Employees can review and update the following basics on their own profile:

- **Personal details** — date of birth, gender, marital status
- **Address** — home address
- **Emergency contact** — name, relationship, and phone number
- **Contact details** — personal phone number (where editable by employee)

Make your changes and save. Changes take effect immediately.

<Callout type="info" title="Contact details">
  Your primary work email is managed by HR or an admin — you can't change it
  yourself. If it's wrong, let HR know.
</Callout>

## Fields managed by HR or Payroll

Some fields affect payroll calculations or require verification. They are
**restricted or masked** for your own protection:

| Field | Who can edit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TIN (Tax Identification Number) | HR Admin / Payroll Admin | Affects statutory deductions |
| NIS / Social Security Number | HR Admin / Payroll Admin | Masked for non-payroll roles |
| Dependent children | HR Admin / Payroll Admin | Affects child allowance in payroll |
| Bank account details | HR Admin / Payroll Admin | Masked at API level; encrypted at rest roadmap |
| Employment status | HR Admin | Hire date, job title, department |
| Contract and salary | HR Admin / Payroll Admin | Governs pay calculations |

You can **see** that these fields exist on your profile, but their values are
masked or hidden unless you hold an HR or Payroll Admin role.

<Callout type="warn" title="Statutory data and pay">
  Fields like TIN, NIS, and dependent children directly affect what's deducted
  from your pay. If any of these look wrong (where you can see them), or if
  a colleague on the right role tells you there's an issue, report it to HR
  immediately so it can be corrected before the next payroll run.
</Callout>

## Reporting incorrect data

If something on your profile is wrong and you can't edit it yourself:

<Steps>
<Step>Note the field name and the incorrect value (or what's missing).</Step>
<Step>Contact HR directly. Give them your employee name or record number so they can locate you quickly.</Step>
<Step>HR will update the field and confirm the change. Payroll-affecting corrections go through a proper, audited process.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Historical records">
  If data carried over from the previous system looks wrong — payroll history,
  leave balances, or attendance records — report it to HR. Historical records
  are preserved as-is for audit, and corrections are applied going forward
  through the corrections workflow.
</Callout>

## Role notes

**Employee view**

You see your personal details, address, emergency contact, and your own payslips
and leave records. Statutory and banking fields are masked or hidden.

**HR Admin view**

HR Admins can view and edit all employee profile fields, including contact
details, employment info, and statutory data. They can also see the fields
employees cannot.

**Payroll Admin view**

Payroll Admins can view and edit statutory fields (TIN, NIS, dependent children)
and banking details. They have full access to the data needed to run payroll
correctly.

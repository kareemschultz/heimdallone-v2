Not every business pays overtime, and not every business pays it the same way.
**Overtime handling** is a single tenant-wide setting that decides what happens
to time worked beyond an employee's scheduled shift.

Set it under **Payroll → Settings → Overtime → Overtime handling**.

<Callout type="info" title="Who can change this">
  Only Payroll Admin and Tenant Owner / Admin can change overtime handling. It
  applies to the whole organization.
</Callout>

## The modes

| Mode | What it does | Hourly staff | Monthly (salaried) staff |
|------|--------------|--------------|--------------------------|
| **Pay overtime premium** *(default)* | Approved overtime is paid using the multipliers below (weekday, Saturday, Sunday, public holiday). | Extra hours paid at the multiplier. | Premium added on top of the fixed salary. |
| **Straight time — no premium** | No overtime premium is paid, and the overtime line is **hidden** on payslips. | Hours are still paid, but at the **flat** rate (no extra). | Salary is unchanged. |

<Callout type="info" title="Coming next — cap at shift">
  A third mode, **No overtime — cap at shift** (pay only the scheduled hours and
  not time worked past shift end), is planned. It is not selectable yet because
  it changes hourly pay and is being validated against real payroll data first.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Example — a 'no overtime' company">
  A company that does not do overtime sets **Straight time — no premium**. An
  hourly employee who works extra is still paid for the hours they worked, but
  with no 1.5× premium, and no overtime line appears on the payslip.
</Callout>

## Long days and time off in lieu (TOIL)

A common field-work pattern is a very long day followed by a day off to balance
the period (for example, 17 hours one day, then the next day off).

- **Monthly (salaried) staff:** pay is the fixed salary, so a long day and a
  lieu day off **balance automatically**. Record the real hours on the long day,
  and mark the next day as an **approved day off** so it is not counted as an
  unpaid absence.
- **Hourly staff:** under **Straight time**, the long day is paid flat and the
  lieu day is unpaid. A built-in **lieu-hours bank** that carries the surplus
  onto a paid lieu day is a planned enhancement.

<Callout type="warning" title="Never enter a fabricated shift">
  Do not record a fake "8-hour shift" for a day someone did not work, or a fake
  shift for a long day. Record the **real** hours and mark rest / lieu days as
  approved days off. Fabricated attendance breaks the audit trail.
</Callout>

## Notes

- The default is **Pay overtime premium**, so existing organizations are
  unchanged unless an admin selects another mode.
- The overtime multipliers (weekday / Saturday / Sunday / public holiday) only
  apply in **Pay overtime premium** mode.
- This setting is organization-wide. Per-shift overtime rules (for example,
  office staff capped at shift while field staff bank long days) are a planned
  enhancement.

Go to **App → Leave** to submit a leave request, track its status, or view your
leave balance. Employees submit requests directly; managers and HR approve them.

## Requesting leave

<Steps>
<Step>Navigate to **App → Leave** and select **Request leave**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **leave type** (annual, sick, casual, or others configured for your organisation).</Step>
<Step>Set the **start date** and **end date**.</Step>
<Step>If the first or last day is a partial day, select whether it's a **first-half** or **second-half** day. You can set this independently for the start and end dates.</Step>
<Step>Add a **description** if required. Some leave types may make this mandatory.</Step>
<Step>Attach a supporting document if relevant (for example, a medical certificate for extended sick leave). Attachments are optional unless your HR policy requires them.</Step>
<Step>Submit the request. It moves to **requested** status and your manager or HR is notified.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Day count is calculated on the server">
  The number of leave days is computed by Heimdallone based on your working
  calendar — non-working days and public holidays are excluded automatically. The
  preview in the form is for guidance; the server's count is what's deducted from
  your balance. See [Leave rules & day counting](/docs/time/leave-effective-dating)
  for how this works.
</Callout>

## Half-days

Select **first half** or **second half** on the start or end date of your request
to take only part of a day. You can combine these — for example, a request from
Monday afternoon to Wednesday morning counts as two full days and two half-days,
with the total calculated against your working schedule.

## Checking your balance

Go to **App → Leave → Balance** to see:

- **Available** — the days remaining for each leave type in the current period.
- **Used** — days taken so far this period (approved leave only).
- **Carry-forward** — any days carried over from the previous period.
- **Next reset** — when the balance resets for the new period.

Pending (not yet approved) requests are shown separately and don't reduce your
available balance until the request is approved.

## Leave statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| requested | The request has been submitted and is waiting for a decision. |
| approved | A manager or HR has approved the request. The days are deducted from your balance. |
| rejected | The request was declined. The days are returned to your balance. The rejection may include a reason. |
| cancelled | The request was cancelled, either by you before approval or by HR after approval. The days are returned to your balance. |

## Approval flow

<Tabs items={["Employee", "Manager", "HR"]}>
<Tab value="Employee">
Submit the request from **App → Leave → Request leave**. You can cancel a request
while it's still in **requested** status. If it's already **approved**, contact HR
to cancel it.

Track the status of your requests from the leave list view. You'll receive a
notification when the decision is made.
</Tab>
<Tab value="Manager">
Pending requests from your direct reports appear in your Leave queue. Select a
request to review the dates, day count, and any note or attachment. Approve or
reject from the request detail. If you reject, you can include a reason.

You can't approve your own leave requests — those go to HR.
</Tab>
<Tab value="HR">
You can approve or reject any request across the organisation. You can also cancel
approved leave when circumstances change. Leave policies (types, entitlements,
carry-forward rules, and accrual) are configured in **App → Settings → Leave
policies**. Changing a policy applies from its effective date only — past approved
requests are governed by the policy that was in force at the time of the request.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Leave policies

Leave policies control what leave types exist, how many days employees are
entitled to, whether carry-forward is allowed and capped, and how days accrue.
Policies are configured by HR and are **effective-dated** — a policy change doesn't
rewrite how previous requests were governed.

See [Leave rules & day counting](/docs/time/leave-effective-dating) for details
on how policies resolve by date and how day counts are computed.

## What to check

- **Balance looks wrong** — check the Used column against your approved requests.
  If a recently approved or cancelled request hasn't updated the balance, wait a
  moment and refresh. If the discrepancy persists, contact HR.
- **Request is stuck in "requested"** — the request is waiting for your manager or
  HR to review it. If it's been pending for longer than expected, follow up with
  your manager directly.
- **Can't request a leave type** — your account may not be assigned to a policy
  that includes that leave type, or the type may not be configured for your
  organisation. Contact HR.

## Troubleshooting

**I submitted a request for the wrong dates**

Cancel the request from **App → Leave** while it's still in **requested** status,
then submit a corrected one. If the request is already approved, contact HR to
cancel and resubmit.

**My available days didn't update after approval**

Approved leave deducts from Available. Refresh the page. If the balance still
looks wrong, check whether the leave type uses accrual (balance builds up over
time) — your entitlement may not have accrued enough days yet. Contact HR to
confirm your current entitlement.

**I was rejected but no reason was given**

The approver may not have included a reason. Contact your manager or HR directly
for clarification.

**My half-day request shows more or fewer days than expected**

The day count is computed from your working calendar, not calendar days. A
half-day on a non-working day counts as zero. The leave-day calculation page
explains the full logic: [Leave rules & day counting](/docs/time/leave-effective-dating).

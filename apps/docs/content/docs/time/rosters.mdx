## What a roster does

A **roster** is a per-date assignment: it records which employee is working which
shift on a specific date. That assignment is the starting point for attendance
calculation — the shift tells the system what scheduled start, end, and minimum
minutes to expect, and the shift's pay rules determine how overtime, lateness, and
premiums are classified.

Rosters and shifts are separate. Shifts are the reusable schedule templates
(defined in **App → Settings → Shifts**). Rosters are the per-date application
of those templates to specific employees.

## Override types

A roster entry can carry an **override** that replaces or modifies the default
shift for that specific date:

| Override | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| None | The employee works their assigned shift as configured. |
| Custom hours | A different start or end time applies for this date only. The shift's pay rules still apply unless a specific shift rule overrides them. |
| Day off | The employee is not working on this date, even if the shift schedule would normally require it. |
| Swap | The employee has swapped this date's shift with another employee or another date. |

## Approval

Roster changes go through an approval step before they take effect. A manager or
HR reviews and approves (or rejects) the proposed change. Until it's approved, the
original assignment remains in force.

## How it feeds attendance and payroll

<Steps>
<Step>**Roster assignment** — the system knows which shift an employee is expected to work on a given date.</Step>
<Step>**Shift definition** — the shift provides scheduled start, end, and minimum required minutes (set in **App → Settings → Shifts**).</Step>
<Step>**Shift rules** — pay-affecting rules (overtime thresholds, grace, night differential, weekend multipliers) are resolved from the shift rule effective on that date. See [Work schedules & shift rules](/docs/time/work-schedules).</Step>
<Step>**Attendance** — punches are compared against the roster. Worked, payable, overtime, late, and early-leave minutes are derived from the rostered shift and the applied rules.</Step>
<Step>**Payroll** — approved attendance records (informed by the roster) feed the payroll engine. Roster and rules never write payroll directly — they influence it through the attendance record.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  An employee without a roster assignment on a given date falls back to the
  organisation's default schedule settings. If an employee's hours are
  consistently misclassified, check whether a roster assignment exists for the
  relevant dates.
</Callout>

## Shifts live in Settings

The shift templates themselves — scheduled start and end times, minimum daily
minutes, and the link to a shift rule — are configured in **App → Settings →
Shifts**, not in the roster itself. The roster references a shift by name; the
shift carries the detail.

Pay-affecting rules for each shift (overtime thresholds, grace windows, break
deductions, night differential, Saturday/Sunday/holiday multipliers) are
effective-dated. Changing a rule going forward doesn't affect how past dates were
calculated. See [Work schedules & shift rules](/docs/time/work-schedules) for
how those rules work.

## Role notes

| Who | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| HR Admin | Full access — create, edit, approve, and archive roster entries and shift assignments. |
| Manager | Can manage roster **assignments** for direct reports but cannot edit shift pay rules (those are HR/payroll only). |
| Employee | No roster management access. Roster data informs your attendance records, which you can view under **App → Attendance**. |

## What to check

- **Attendance record shows wrong shift** — the roster assignment for that date may
  point to the wrong shift, or no assignment exists and the system fell back to the
  default. Contact HR to review the roster entry.
- **An override isn't reflected in attendance** — check whether the override was
  approved. An unapproved override doesn't take effect.
- **A day-off roster entry still shows an "absent" attendance record** — attendance
  records are updated when punches are processed. If the day-off override was
  approved after attendance was already calculated, ask HR to recalculate the record.

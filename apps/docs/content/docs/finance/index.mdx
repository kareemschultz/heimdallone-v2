Finance is a **coordination layer**: it reads payroll actuals and approved project time to produce cost reports and budgets, and owns the general ledger. It links to other modules but never writes to them.

Go to **App → Finance**. The Finance area is visible to Payroll Admin, HR Admin, Tenant Owner/Admin, Auditor, and Managers (scoped to their departments).

<Callout type="info" title="Guardrail">
  Finance reads payroll and project records. It never writes to them. Cost reports
  are read models; budget entries don't affect payroll calculations; GL payroll
  postings are derived from confirmed payslips, not the other way round.
</Callout>
